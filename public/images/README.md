# Custom Images for Hosting (Option 1)

Place your photo files here with the following filenames to permanently embed them into the website:

### You're My Scrapbook Page
- `youre-my-main.jpg` (or .png / .webp) - The main couple photo

### Dear Love Film Strip Page
- `film-1.jpg` - Film strip photo 1 (top left)
- `film-2.jpg` - Film strip photo 2 (top right)
- `film-3.jpg` - Film strip photo 3 (bottom left)
- `film-4.jpg` - Film strip photo 4 (bottom right)

### Love You Keepsake Collage Page
- `keepsake-1.jpg` to `keepsake-12.jpg` - Collage photos 1 through 12

When hosted on any web service, these photos are served to all visitors automatically!
